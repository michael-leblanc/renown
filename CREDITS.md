## Made with AI tools (for the Steam AI disclosure)

Steam asks what in a game was made with AI and how. The sources marked `ai_generated` in the wardrobe:

- **Orc base body (orc_model_1)**: the orc's body (a bare body with shorts) was generated as a 3D model by Tripo from a text prompt (Michael, 2026-11-24); we rigged it, recoloured it and render it into the game's 2D sprites.
- **Chest plate with pauldrons ("sample")**: a chest plate generated as a 3D model with TRELLIS from a picture of a Tripo3D model (2026-11-23); on trial.
- **Long plate armour ("armor")**: a long plate armour generated as a 3D model with AI tools (TRELLIS or Tripo3D) from a text prompt (2026-11-23); on trial.
- **Goblin scrap armour ("goblin_armor")**: the goblin scrap armour, generated as a 3D model with AI tools (TRELLIS or Tripo3D) from a text prompt, cut into pieces and fitted by us (approved 2026-11-24).
