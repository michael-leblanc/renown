## Review: PASS
1. **The world's click handler fights PRO-005's click-to-walk.** See prototype/world/world.gd:194-217. `_unhandled_input` toggles the door on any left click on the door tile, or on any drawn pixel of the closed door. Those pixels also cover the inside tile behind the door. So in PRO-005, a click to walk to the doorway, or to the room behind it, opens or closes the door instead of (or as well as) walking. PRO-005's own rule is "door opens when the human reaches it". `_process` also pans the world's own Camera2D with WASD and the arrow keys, and that camera is current. PRO-005 can't edit `prototype/world`, so it must call `world.set_process_unhandled_input(false)`, `world.set_process(false)` and `make_current()` on its own camera. Put that in PRO-005's notes, or add an `interactive` flag here before merging.
2. **Walls are drawn on the building's outer edge, but the whole ring of tiles blocks walking.** See prototype/world/world_gen.gd:96-101 and world.gd:130-140. The visible floor is 5 by 4, but only the 3 by 2 middle and the door tile are walkable. In PRO-005 the human will stop a full tile short of every inside wall, while outside it walks right up to the wall face. That is acceptable for this task, and is the kind of thing the test game should find. But it shows that D188's "blocks walking" as a flag on each tile does not fit this tileset's edge-drawn walls. Record it as a lesson (D201) before SIM-002 copies the per-tile model. Don't paper over it in PRO-005.
