# UAT PRO-006 — Click the dummy to start an auto-attack fight that lasts about 30 seconds

⏱ ~20 min · 📍 the test game, four scenes (commands below), run from the repo root

## What changed
You can click an orc dummy in the patch and fight it, and the whole test game can now be looked at and felt in one sitting.

## Before you start
- Run the commands from the repo root. First time only: `scripts/godot.sh --headless --path prototype --import`
- Look part, in this order (each opens a window, close it to go on):
  - Patch, door, draw order: `scripts/godot.sh --path prototype res://world/world.tscn` (arrows pan, wheel zooms, click the door)
  - Three races and their sizes: `scripts/godot.sh --path prototype res://character/character.tscn` (Left/Right turn, Space walk/stand, O orc size, G goblin size)
  - The five block candidates: `scripts/godot.sh --path prototype res://block/block.tscn` (1 to 5 replay one, Left/Right turn)
- Feel part: `scripts/godot.sh --path prototype res://play/play.tscn`

## Test it
- [ ] **World scene: pan round the building, zoom with the wheel, click the door** → do the wall, trees and rocks draw in a believable order, and does the door open and close?
- [ ] **Character scene: turn all 8 directions, press Space, O and G** → are the human, the orc (1.4 x 1.2) and the goblin (0.75) sized right next to each other?
- [ ] **Block scene: watch the five, press 1 to 5** → judge the block LOOK here, at full length: which reads best as "I blocked it" (1 CrouchIdle, 2 TakeDamage start, 3 white flash, 4 Special1, 5 Taunt), with its sound?
- [ ] **Play scene: click the ground far away, then press ] and [ a few times** → is 3.3 tiles a second (0.30 s) right, or what should it be?
- [ ] **Play scene: pick a block with 1 to 5, then click the orc dummy** → the human walks beside it, both swing every 2 s, a hit plays the flinch, a miss plays your block, bars and the fight timer show. In the fight a block is cut off after 0.5 s by the defender's next swing; for all five in full press L 8 times (swing 4 s) and M 4 times (offset 2 s), 0.25 s a press.
- [ ] **Let a fight finish, press R, fight again twice** → how long was each (the timer stops on the last blow): too long, too short or about right?
- [ ] **Press G, then walk past a tree or a wall corner** → G switches between D185's own diagonal rule (default) and the stricter one; which feels better?
- [ ] **Tune live: Z X health, C V damage, B N chance to hit, K L swing time** → the new number shows on screen and the next swing or fight uses it.

## Try to break it
- Click the ground in the middle of a fight (the human walks away, healths stay, click the dummy to carry on), click the dummy behind a tree, click a dead dummy (nothing until R).

## Not in this task
- The dummy never walks or fights back by moving, it only swings; no dodge, no armour, no damage range (D172), no skills growing, no regeneration.
- The block has no real animation (D163): the five candidates are stand-ins. Characters are not obstacles (D187): the human can overlap the dummy's sprite.

## Bring back
- What to change: walk speed, which block (1 to 5), whether about 30 s feels right (a 20-fight run gives a 28.9 s average, the true mean at these numbers is about 26 s), the sizes of the three races, the draw order, the diagonal rule (default or G).
