## Design
- How many NPCs is "a lot"? The full-detail budget is 500 on desktop 1, with stand-ins (D217); `scripts/measure.sh` measures again when real rules arrive. After D121 each character on screen is about 8 to 10 stacked part images, not one: the client wave's F3 overlay shows frames a second with 34 characters.
- Hosting (D197 to D199): settled. Still open: whether port forwarding works through Michael's two routers, tried only once friends join.
- First build (D179, D184, D185): how far does a character see (16 tiles is a stand-in)? Is 20 ms still the right share of a tick once combat, clients and saving exist? `scripts/godot.sh` runs on Linux and macOS (Michael played the test game on his Mac); Windows has none yet (D177). A path search on edges averages 18.5 ms across about 400 tiles (SIM-004): is D192's cap enough once roamers cross the island? A wall going up or down relabels the whole map, about 4.7 s on 512 by 512 (D203): it needs a cheaper update before anything changes walls during play.
- NPC brain (D181): the slice's action list (Claude's proposal, not shown to Michael one by one: stand at a post, wander, serve the shop, talk, fight, back off, run to the guards and tell, pick up loot, use a bandage), and the needs' numbers.
- Art (D121, D205): The unarmoured Creator orc is a plain green figure with no ears or face: is that good enough, and does the orc and goblin pack come back later (D121 is "for now")? Does size vary from one orc to another, with Strength for example (D114)? Do orcs and goblins share one green or get different skin colours, and what skin colours do humans get? Are Zombie 01 to 10 modern (D81)? Which Creator head parts get the "covers face" property (D40; candidates Head13, Head14, Head19, Head20)? Are goblin wolf riders wanted later (the Creator has a wolf mount)? Skeletons can use the Creator too (it has a skeleton body); demons and zombies are pre-baked sheets, so what do they look like when their gear changes (D80)? Is Shield4's painted cross (an emblem) right for this world? What are `Attack2`, `Attack4`, `Attack5` for, and is there an attack-while-running animation (D170)? `Effect1` to `Effect5` for magic.
- War (D14): how and when does it soften? How much does each good deed bring back (D60)? When do patrols and raids arrive (D53)?
- NPC judgement (D28): how are gear and fame weighed into "looks stronger"? Do numbers count? What does a roamer do once home (D56)? Does an orc NPC weigh body size and weapon size more than others do, and so get fooled more often (D114)?
- Ownership (D32, D35): how fast does a shop's stock refill in the slice, and with what (D141)? What if a place has several residents and one dies? Do shopkeepers and townsfolk ever leave their shop or home in the slice?
- Property (D36, D37): can a reborn player buy back their old house to get its chest (a paid stash across death)? Do NPC shop prices start from an item's value? Who sells while the owner is away or offline (a hired NPC shopkeeper)? How do NPCs earn money and choose what to buy? Can NPCs buy property too (D4)? What does a building cost, and who is paid?
- Shop buying (D143): can a buy order ask for a condition (D138) or only an item name? Does standing (D9) change what an NPC shop pays you? Can a player owner set different prices by faction or standing, or refuse to sell to someone? Does a sell order have a count, or sell all it has?
- Reputation: how far does each crime move standing and a trait? Do Morality and Mercy drift back toward the middle? The thresholds for guards and bounties? What does yielding look like (needs a "beaten but alive" state)? Who pays a bounty and how is it claimed? What makes a legend, and what does the world do about one beyond bounties? How much does one NPC remember?
- Combat numbers (D165, D171, D172): everything in D172 is a starting point to balance in play. How is a partial set of armour counted (chest, head, legs)? Do spears and arrows take a metal for their head (D133)? Does health ever grow, if stats do (D19)? When does each weapon kind get its own speed and damage (D171)?
- Running (D52, D170, D228, D229): does a hated target (a known murderer) get chased further? How is the town's edge marked on generated land? How far does a bow reach, and can an archer shoot while running? How does a partial metal set count for run energy? Does a mount ever beat the run?
- Healing (D164, D168, D169): can a bandage be used on someone else? How fast does health come back out of a fight, and what counts as "out of a fight"? Do NPCs use bandages (D4)?
- Death extras: does a hidden stash or the uncovered map survive? Does running from a fight cost anything? PvP rules? The fallen state's rules (after the slice, D26)?
- Legacy (D148, D149): proposed to Michael and not answered, for after the slice: NPCs tell tales of the notable dead, players and NPCs alike (D4), under "What do people say here?" (D91), and a tale lasts longer the more Fame the dead had (D7). Is there a list of past lives the player can look back at (a Legends view, as in Dwarf Fortress), and does it include dead NPCs? Does the legacy name the killer, when the dead character never learned the name (D31)?
- Chat (D150): how far does local speech carry? Can the enemy faction read it? When global chat arrives, does it show a name to people who don't have it (D29), and does it pass on news a character couldn't know (D22)?
- Looting (D142): after the slice, how long does each kind of item take, and is a looter interrupted by being hit? Can two characters loot the same corpse at once? Is looting a body seen by a witness a deed of its own (looting your own dead is not in the slice, D32)?
- Living NPCs: which jobs, and how does an NPC pick one? What extra rank does an NPC get for killing a player? Replacement (D15, D47): the delay, the newcomer's starting gear, how soon a role is filled and with what gear, and are animals replaced when killed (D98)?
- World simulation (D183): how long do loot and a body stay before they disappear? How much faster do newcomers come to a town low on people? What are the limits the fast-forward test checks (population, items, the strongest character)? Does an NPC far stronger than everyone need any answer beyond players teaming up (D135)?
- Factions: beyond the slice's two (D12, D77), which factions and sub-factions exist, which races are in each, and which are good or evil? What does a people change beyond D96 and D97: the numbers and overlap, Agility and Endurance, and the odds of a background or a talent? Does a background shift the stats?
- Backgrounds (D152, D157): do a town and a race change the odds later? Does Oldscar have its own word for a Well-woken (D158)? Does an NPC's role at the start follow its background (guards are Soldiers, roamers are Hunters, D64, D82)? Do starting skills count against the total cap (D140)? Some backgrounds wake with two skills at 30 and others with one (D152, D166): is that evened out, with gear for example?
- Random birth (D154 to D156): is an Oldscar NPC newcomer an orc or a goblin, half and half (D82)? Do Agility and Endurance ever get a race bonus? Do stats ever grow after birth, or only skills (D19)?
- Generated quests (D129, D130): how much trust does a personal request need, in numbers? Does the notice board exist in the slice, before quests do? Who speaks for a faction (which "officials", and do guilds exist)? What makes one NPC hate another, when nobody has family here (D66)? What does a shop need and where is it found (this also answers where shop stock comes from)? What is the reward, and who pays it? Can NPCs take requests too (D4, and the Vision says some do)? How is a kill proven? Can a request be wrong, out of date, or a trap? Is any kind in the slice, or all after it (D11)?
- Talking to NPCs (D126 to D128): how is the description of someone with no name written? Does the NPC need your name first (D42)? Does it remember that you asked about someone? What does the sheet keep about other people you asked about? The full list of choices in a conversation.
- Generated land (D136, D137): a new lore fact about a place now becomes a rule for the generator, so who checks that the two stay in step? Which buildings does the slice need, and how many (D189)? How simple can the slice's generator be? What does a generated economy need (what NPCs make, need and trade)?
- Party (D135): how does a character recruit an NPC, and what does the NPC want in return? How big can a party be? Does a recruited NPC keep its own will (D4)?
- Power (D134, D135): where does the power come from, and in what share: skills, stats, materials, enchantments, rare spells, talents? What are the skill cap numbers (D140)? How long should it take to get there, given permadeath? How does enchanting work, and where do rare spells come from?
- Wear (D138): is wear in the slice or after it? What happens at zero condition: the item breaks, or it only works badly? Does the condition show on inspect (D131)? Can a character repair their own gear with a skill? Do enchanted items wear too (D134)?
- Items (D132, D133): when do magic grades arrive, and must they be identified? How does an item's value (D37) follow from its material? What are the penalties for gear used below its Strength (D139)?
- Inventory (D122 to D124): do different backpacks add different amounts? Are coins used for house prices and bounties too (D36)? Which other items stack? What happens to items that no longer fit when a backpack is taken off?
- Slice: when does the region grow past D34? Which gear? The skills are D151, the backgrounds D152, and there are no talents (D153). Michael asked whether the Creator can do magic: yes for the basics (3 spell effects, `Magic1` to `Magic3`, drawn on the caster), but it has no casting animation and nothing that flies to a target. Magic comes after the slice (D151). After the slice: what does each talent open, and do a stat, a background or a race change the odds of one (D25)?
- Hearing (D23, D51; after the slice): how often do NPCs talk and how close must a listener be? Does an NPC hold back from someone it distrusts? Can players pass knowledge on?
- Names (D31, D38, D42): the reactions after the slice (a guard that won't let you pass, fear)? Does a shopkeeper also hold back what the town knows? Can a character give a false name? Does "introduce yourself" always get the NPC's name in return? Does a name with no tale attached spread to other towns?
- Inspect and faces (D22, D29, D30): which items hide race or face, and are any in the slice? The art can't settle it (a head is about 8 px wide), so it stays an item property. After D121, does full armour hide race, when size still shows? (Wrong tales: D195; NPCs act on their own memory first: D196.)
- Living world (D54): how often does a new chest appear, what is the maximum, and what is in it (D62)? Do NPCs go looking for chests? How long does an empty chest stay (D61)? Who lives in a camp (a sub-faction or a faction itself, D55)? Can a camp be destroyed, and does it grow? How much world do about 10 players need to each find their own?
- Saving (D27, D182): the format inside the files? Does the save interval need to be shorter than 2 minutes, given a crash can undo a death? Offline (D49): what does the character do during the 30 seconds, and is a lost connection treated like logging off?
- Lore (D65 to D120): Does the One Hand become a sub-faction with its own standing after the slice, how many of a town's NPCs are believers, and how does a newcomer get a creed (D79)? What does "so different" look like in play (D69)? Does orcs thinking little of goblins change anything in play beyond the starting roles (D75, D82)?
